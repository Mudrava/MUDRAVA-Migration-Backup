# Support

MUDRAVA Migration & Backup is free and open source. Support is provided on a
best-effort basis through these channels:

- **WordPress.org support forum** (preferred, public):
  [wordpress.org/support/plugin/mudrava-migration-backup](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/mudrava-migration-backup/)
  - public threads help other site owners and improve our search visibility.
- **Email:** [support@mudrava.com](mailto:support@mudrava.com) for anything
  you would rather keep private.
- **Security issues:** do not open a public issue. Follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Before you open a ticket

Migration problems are usually host-specific, so please include:

1. WordPress, PHP and plugin versions, and your host.
2. The exact error code shown (codes start with `MUDRAVA_`).
3. Whether the failure happened during export, download, upload, import or
   preflight.
4. Approximate site size (database rows, `wp-content` size, number of files).

Never attach a real archive or paste database credentials to a public thread.

## Response times

We aim to acknowledge public forum threads and email within 2-3 business days.
There is no guaranteed support SLA for the free plugin.

## Commercial help

For large sites, multisite, or a supervised migration, reach out at
[mudrava.com/en/](https://mudrava.com/en/).
